One Night. Eight Stars. One Cause.

Dancing with the Shasta Stars returns for its 17th annual evening of show-stopping entertainment!

Dancing with the Shasta Stars is a Broadway-style production on Saturday, October 17 at the Cascade Theatre in Redding, presented by Obsidian IT and benefiting One SAFE Place.

Eight community leaders take the stage alongside professional dancers to raise funds for survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, and trafficking right here in Shasta County.

About

Fundraisers like Dancing with the Shasta Stars enable One SAFE Place (OSP) to increase community awareness about abuse and expand services for Shasta County's most vulnerable residents. Each ticket purchased significantly impacts the lives of those OSP serves, helping to break the cycle of abuse and move our community towards a safer future.
