Join us a make a difference in our community! At the Children’s Legacy Center, our mission is to disrupt the cycle of violence and trauma through crisis intervention and community education.

Discover meaningful volunteer opportunities where you can make a real difference. At the Children’s Legacy Center, you’ll collaborate with passionate professionals who make challenging days easier and good days exceptional.

We are deeply grateful for your consideration in joining our mission. Be a part of our team, and together, we can bring hope and healing to those who need it most.
