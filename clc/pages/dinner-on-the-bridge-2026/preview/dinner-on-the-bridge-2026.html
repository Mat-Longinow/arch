Ten Years. One Bridge. One Evening.

Every day in Shasta County, a child walks through a door after the worst day of their life. A family arrives when the systems they trusted have failed them. An adult survivor steps forward, ready to begin healing. What happens next depends entirely on who is waiting on the other side of that door.

For ten years, the people of Children's Legacy Center have been waiting — forensic interviewers, trauma therapists, family advocates, medical professionals, trafficking response specialists. State and federal contracts pay for the floor of what every child deserves. Philanthropy is what raises the ceiling. This is the first time we have gathered the community around a single table to celebrate what has been built.
