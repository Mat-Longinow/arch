At One Safe Place and the Children’s Legacy Center, we are on a mission to break the cycle of violence and trauma, creating a safer, more compassionate world through crisis intervention and community education. Our commitment is rooted in all-inclusive trauma-informed care, tailored to the unique needs of each individual we serve.

There is a rare gift found when one is a part of something that is far bigger than oneself. Imagine walking alongside a team of passionate, brilliant, and kind-hearted individuals who make challenging days more bearable and good days truly exceptional. Both One Safe Place and the Children’s Legacy Center understand the value of investing in our organizations' health, nurturing our staff's well-being, and fostering a culture of growth.

We are looking for committed, humble, strategic, and passionate individuals who possess integrity in all areas. With the incredible collaboration of our partners, we have accomplished significant goals, such as reducing trauma during the prosecutorial process for children and discovering evidence-based practices for healing exploited youth. Join us as we expand our impact and continue to innovate.

Together, One Safe Place and the Children’s Legacy Center are dedicated to serving our community with excellence and dedication, leaving a lasting legacy in the lives of those we support.
